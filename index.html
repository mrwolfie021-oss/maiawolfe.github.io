I'm a Marketing major at The Ohio State University's Max M. Fisher College of Business, with a minor in Business Analytics and a place in the University Honors program.

I'm drawn to marketing because it sits at the meeting point of data and storytelling — figuring out what an audience actually needs, and then communicating it in a way that earns their attention. Right now I'm exploring that through brand strategy, consumer behavior, and digital media, with a particular interest in how analytics can sharpen creative work rather than dilute it.

Currently, I'm a Marketing & Communications Intern at Parallax Advanced Research, on the Marketing Team for Fisher Ink Magazine, and on the Events Committee for the OSU chapter of the American Marketing Association. Off the clock, I'm probably reading mystery fiction, playing guitar, or captaining intramural soccer.
